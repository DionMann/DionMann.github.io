MATH 1070Q: Mathematics for Business and Economics

Materials from my Summer 2026 offering of MATH 1070Q at UConn (CAPS/SSS program), organized by topic.

Course at a Glance

  • Content: linear equations, ratios and percents, simple and compound interest, sets, counting, probability, functions, systems of linear equations, and matrices.
  • Textbook: Contemporary Mathematics (OpenStax), free online. Access textbook. Section numbers in the table below refer to this text.
  • Format: 16 topics in a five-week summer session, with 110-minute classes Monday through Friday. Each class day introduces one topic, except for Mondays which are assessment days.
  • Assessment: Second Chance Grading (O. E. Fernandez). Each topic gets two graded attempts: a 20-minute, four-question quiz the morning after it is taught, and a retest with alternate questions on a Monday Second Chance exam. The topic grade is the higher of the two. Course grade: topics 60%, worksheets 20%, final exam 20%.
  • Worksheets: a four-question exit ticket during the last 20 minutes of each topic day, graded on completion.
  • Homework: optional weekly practice sets, one per four topics. Quizzes and exams draw from them, which the students know.
