I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Connecticut, advised by Ambar Sengupta. I received my B.A. in mathematics and physics with honors from the University of Colorado Boulder in 2023, where I wrote an honors thesis on deformation quantization under Markus Pflaum.

My research is in differential topology and mathematical physics, focusing mostly on gauge theory: the study of connections on principal bundles. Much of my current work uses diffeology to extend these ideas to path spaces and other infinite-dimensional spaces. More about my work is on the Research page, and slides from my talks are on the Presentations page.

I also teach at UConn as an instructor of record. In 2026–2027 I am a Math Teaching Innovation Fellow teaching Calculus I, and in Summer 2026 I designed and taught MATH 1070Q for UConn’s summer bridge program. Details are on my Teaching page and CV.
