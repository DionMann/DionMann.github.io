Instructor Resources

This page collects the materials I have written for courses I teach at UConn, organized by course and by topic. Instructors are welcome to use and adapt them in their own classes. (Students looking for a current course page should go to the Teaching page.)

Courses

MATH 1131Q: Calculus I In progress

Single-variable calculus: limits and continuity, derivatives and their applications, and an introduction to integration, including the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

Posted: materials will be added during Fall 2026 and Spring 2027.

MATH 1070Q: Mathematics for Business and Economics Available

Finite mathematics with applications to business: linear equations, ratios and percents, simple and compound interest, sets, counting, probability, functions, systems of linear equations, and matrices. Sixteen topics, built around Second Chance Grading and a free OpenStax textbook.

Posted: lecture slides (Topics 1–13), homework (Topics 1–16), syllabus, and course schedule.

MATH 1020Q: Introduction to Problem Solving In progress

Materials from my Spring 2025, Fall 2025, and Spring 2026 offerings.

Posted: coming soon.

What You Will Find

Each course page lists materials topic by topic, so you can take a single lecture or an entire unit.

  • Lecture slides: the slides as presented in class (PDF).
  • Lecture notes: written notes that accompany the lectures.
  • Homework: problem sets, keyed to textbook sections where possible.
  • Worksheets and activities: in-class worksheets, exit tickets, and group activities.
  • Course documents: syllabi and schedules, showing how each course was structured and graded.
